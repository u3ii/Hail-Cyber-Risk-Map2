Recommended Actions

Prioritized actions derived from the asset register and dependency analysis.

  • Prioritize Critical and High-Risk Assets Ensure Critical and High-rated assets are covered by the highest-priority monitoring, isolated backups, and incident response routines.
  • Strengthen Shared Dependencies Assets with multiple dependents produce the largest cascading impact. Redundancy and failover plans should be reviewed for these components.
  • Enforce Multi-Factor Authentication Apply MFA across all administrative and employee-facing access points to reduce credential compromise risk.
  • Schedule Periodic Risk Reviews Keep the registry aligned with infrastructure changes through recurring risk assessments and dependency audits.
